## What is EFC?

Energy-Flow Cosmology (EFC) begins with a simple organising proposition:

> Energy flows along entropy gradients.

The framework investigates how distributions of energy, entropy gradients, information constraints and flows can give rise to structure and observable dynamics. Its formal cosmological programme asks whether gravitational and large-scale phenomena can be described through entropy-dependent energy-flow dynamics. Its wider conceptual programme examines whether related mathematical structures can help describe cognition, artificial intelligence, consciousness-related models and the development of civilisation.

EFC is therefore presented here at two connected but distinct levels:

1. **The physical theory:** formal models, equations, regimes, cosmological and galactic observables, numerical pipelines and falsifiable predictions.
2. **The meta-framework:** a cross-domain map for comparing how energy, entropy, information, structure, feedback and emergence appear in different classes of systems.

A shared vocabulary does not by itself prove that the same physical mechanism operates in every domain. Each cross-domain connection needs its own definitions, mapping and evidence.

```text
COSMOS
  └─ physical fields, energy distributions and entropy gradients
       └─ REGIMES
            └─ different scale- and state-dependent behaviours
                 └─ PROXIES
                      └─ observables, measurements and model parameters

INFORMATION ↔ ENERGY FLOW ↔ ENTROPY
       └─ structure, dynamics, feedback and emergence
            ├─ physical systems
            ├─ biological and neural systems
            ├─ cognition and consciousness models
            ├─ artificial intelligence / ASI research
            └─ human systems and civilisation
                    └─ HOMO FLUXUS
```

This is a conceptual navigation map, not a claim that every arrow is already a derived physical law.

## How to read the claims

The wiki distinguishes four kinds of statement:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Formal** | Defined in a mathematical specification or model. |
| **Derived** | Follows from stated assumptions and derivation steps. |
| **Empirical** | Evaluated against identified data with a reproducible method. |
| **Hypothesis / programme** | A proposed connection or prediction that still needs testing. |

The status of a claim may differ between papers and model versions. Read the cited source and its date before treating a number as current.

## Source-of-truth rule

This wiki explains and connects the material. It does not replace it.

For technical or scientific decisions, use this order:

1. Current versioned specification and source code.
2. The relevant paper, data package and DOI.
3. The validation ledger and its linked reproducibility material.
4. This wiki as an explanatory guide.

Repository-specific rules on provenance, evidence classes, pre-registration and claim language remain binding. In particular, external research may be described as *consistent with* or *overlapping with* an EFC prediction; it must not be described as confirming EFC merely because the result is compatible.
