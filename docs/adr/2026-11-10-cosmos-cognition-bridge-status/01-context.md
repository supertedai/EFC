## Field ownership (the bridge convention)

`scripts/maintenance/efc_bro_konvensjon.py` splits node fields into `MOTOR_EIDE`
(written from the engine by `efc_bro_synk.py --skriv`) and `ATLAS_EIDE` (curated; the
atlas is the source). Measured:

- `/ontology/proveniens` **is** `ATLAS_EIDE` → the atlas owns it; an edit here is the
  correct side and is not overwritten by a sync.
- `/epistemikk` **is** `ATLAS_EIDE` → same, and this change deliberately does not touch
  it.
- `/revisjon` appears in **neither** table. Per the convention docstring, that is a hole
  only if the *engine* emits the field. Measured baseline:
  `efc_bro_synk.py --sjekk` → `ingen avvik mellom motor og atlas`, rc=0, so no engine
  emits it and the atlas carries it alone. `--skriv` writes only `MOTOR_EIDE` paths, so
  an atlas-side edit to `revisjon` cannot be reverted by a sync.

## Coverage register — a measured tension with the card

`doi_coverage_register.py` classifies each canonical DOI (`klass = "atlas_scope" if
is_empirical else "changelog"`) and derives `disposition` (`atlas_candidate` only when
`atlas_scope` **and** not yet in the atlas). Measured rows:

| DOI | class | disposition | in atlas union (before) | in `doi_coverage_pending.json` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `31940547` (B1/B2) | `changelog` | `changelog_only` | false | no |
| `31969983` (S→Ω̂,κ̂) | `changelog` | `changelog_only` | false | no |
| `31940505` (EFC-C v0.1) | `changelog` | `changelog_only` | false | no |
| `32091700` (EFC-C v2.x) | `changelog` | `changelog_only` | false | no |
| `31289806` (field resonance) | **`atlas_scope`** | **`atlas_candidate`** | false | **yes** (backlog of 60) |

Two consequences, recorded rather than glossed:

1. The repo's own register classes the two bridge **proposals** as `changelog`-class, not
   `atlas_scope`. This change puts them into the atlas anyway, on Morten's explicit
   mandate. The row's `class` does not change, so `disposition` stays `changelog_only` and
   no backlog item is silently consumed. This disagreement is real and is left visible in
   the diff.
2. `31289806` is `atlas_candidate` — a DOI queued for a **human/reviewed semantic node
   mapping**. Writing its DOI string into the bank would flip it to `atlas_covered` on a
   *literal mention*, removing it from the review backlog without anyone deciding the
   mapping. This card does not own that decision, so the DOI is **not** written into the
   bank; the paper is cited by repository path and date instead (see `03-decision.md`).

## Primary sources re-verified (not the chat summary)

All read from the local PDFs/packages under `docs/papers/efc/`, with DOI existence and
title checked against DataCite (`efc_doi`).

- **`10.6084/m9.figshare.31940505`** — EFC-C v0.1, 2026-03-31, DOI registered 2026-03-31.
  States as a limitation: *"The mapping from EFC cosmological parameters to neural entropy
  production rates requires a bridge theory that has not yet been derived"*, and *"EFC-C
  has not yet been fit to a neural dataset."* This is the **earlier** state.
- **`10.6084/m9.figshare.31940547`** — v0.2, DataCite registered 2026-04-05. Proposes two
  bridge equations: **B1** cosmological entropy-production → neural analogue (revised to
  degree-heterogeneity control), **B2** neural entropy tensor ↔ RLHF reward-landscape
  curvature with `β_KL ↔ 1/T_cog`. Its own limitations: *"B2 is exact at the level of
  equilibrium optima … Transient dynamics … require an additional kinetic theory"*;
  *"Tcog requires independent measurement. Until Tcog is directly extracted from fMRI data,
  Eq. (13) is a consistency relation, not a standalone prediction"*; *"The bridge does not
  close the cosmology–RLHF loop directly."* Its "next steps" are **to test** P4\*\*/P5.
- **`10.6084/m9.figshare.31969983`** — v1.0, DataCite registered 2026-04-09. Proposes a
  dimensionless, regime-indexed mapping `S → (Ω̂, κ̂)` with coupled dynamics and a
  one-parameter non-separable functional. Its limitations, verbatim: *"The identification
  δS/δρ ↔ transfer entropy in the cognitive regime is an ansatz, not a derivation"*;
  *"γ is currently unconstrained; its value must come from data"*; and on the propofol
  material: *"These results do not yet constitute a direct test of the full model."* The
  README's framing ("closes the cosmology-to-cognition bridge") is the authors' framing;
  the limitations section is the operative evidence status.
- **`10.6084/m9.figshare.31289806`** — 2026-02-08. Empirical content is propofol-EEG only;
  its cosmological statement is explicitly an analogy: *"This is not identity — it is the
  same class of thermodynamic arc."* It calls its own effect *"a candidate hypothesis, not a
  confirmed effect."* This is the earlier empirical isolation between the branches.
- **`10.6084/m9.figshare.32091700`** — **unresolved metadata discrepancy**: DataCite
  resolves the DOI to the title *"EFC-C **v2.0**: Quantitative Entropy-Gradient Predictions
  for Cognitive States"*, the local `index.json` says version `2.1` / *"EFC-C **v2.1**:
  Degree-Heterogeneity …"*, and `figshare/doi-map.json` (hence `doi_coverage_matrix.json`)
  also says **v2.1**. Not resolved by this card; recorded as a discrepancy in the bank.
