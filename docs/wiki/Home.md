## What is EFC?

**Core axiom:** energy flows along entropy gradients — this generates spacetime, structure, and awareness.

**Status:** non-rejectable candidate theory. Across 103+ independent tests EFC has not been falsified, but margins are too small to outperform ΛCDM yet. Global verdict: **OPEN**.

**One-line summary of the proposal:** gravity adjusts itself where entropy is still building up; ordinary Einstein gravity everywhere else. One mechanism, two numbers, no invisible particles required.

Full background: [root `README.md`](../../README.md).

## Conventions used in this wiki

- Links to **local files** use relative paths from `docs/wiki/`.
- Links to **DOIs** point to Figshare.
- Papers are referenced by their slug in [`/docs/papers/efc/`](../papers/efc/).
- Pages ending in `.html` are rendered artefacts served via GitHub Pages.
- `⚙️ auto-generated` in a page header means it is regenerated by [`scripts/maintenance/efc_build_wiki.py`](../../scripts/maintenance/efc_build_wiki.py) — do not edit by hand.
