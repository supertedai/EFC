Why (the standard, in brief)

Both an echo chamber (agreeing with yourself) and a consensus cap (deferring to the field) are the same error — measuring truth by agreement. The standard measures it by falsifiability against data instead, and applies identically to EFC and to ΛCDM — consensus gets no free pass. Trust flows only across a paradigm boundary (cross-paradigm survival, novel confirmed predictions, out-of-sample data); self-sealing passes count for nothing. A frontier claim must reproduce the rival's raw-observable successes before it earns the right to diverge.
