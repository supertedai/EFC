# Attention Schema Theory (AST)

> The brain controls attention; in order to control it well, the brain
> constructs an internal, simplified *model* of its own attention — the
> **attention schema**. That schema, when introspected, *is* what we call
> "awareness." Subjective consciousness is therefore the brain's
> self-description of a real but reduced control-theoretic variable.

**Originator:** Michael S. A. Graziano (Princeton Neuroscience Institute).
**First formal statement:** Graziano & Kastner (2011), *Cognitive
Neuroscience* — "Human consciousness and its relationship to social
neuroscience: a novel hypothesis."
**Mature version:** Graziano (2019), *Rethinking Consciousness*; Graziano,
Guterstam, Bio & Wilterson (2020), *Cognitive Neuropsychology* — "Toward a
standard model of consciousness: reconciling the attention schema, global
workspace, higher-order thought, and illusionist theories."

---

## 1. The control-theoretic core

In engineering, any controller that regulates a process needs a *model* of
that process — a *schema*. Examples:

- The **body schema** lets the motor system plan around an internal model
  of the body (Head & Holmes 1911; explored by Ramachandran's phantom-limb
  work).
- The **attention schema** lets the cortex plan around an internal model of
  its own attentional state.

The body schema is admittedly inaccurate (it doesn't include the immune
system, gut chemistry, or moment-by-moment skeletal mechanics). Likewise,
the attention schema is a *cartoon* of attention — and that simplification
is *precisely* what makes phenomenal claims feel non-physical:

| What attention really is | What the attention schema reports |
|---|---|
| Distributed competition over thousands of cortical columns | A unified, point-like locus of awareness |
| Statistical gain modulation | A vivid "felt" quality |
| Substrate of synapses & neuromodulators | "Pure, immaterial" experience |
| Causal and physically describable | Mysterious, unmeasurable |

This is AST's explanation of the **hard problem**: the hard problem is not
a fact about phenomenal reality; it is a *predicted property of the
schema's output*. A system that builds such a schema *will* insist it has
ineffable inner experience — because that is what the model says.

## 2. Empirical predictions

| Domain | Prediction | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Right temporo-parietal junction (rTPJ) & superior temporal sulcus (STS)** as substrate of the schema | Damage → loss of awareness of attended content (visual neglect) without loss of attention itself in some cases | Supported (Wilterson et al. 2020, *PNAS*; Graziano lab) |
| **Awareness ≈ attention model, not attention** | One should find dissociations: attention without awareness (subliminal priming) and *reported* awareness without attention (false reports under load) | Replicated in multiple paradigms |
| **Other-minds attribution** | The same machinery models *others'* attention → underwrites social cognition / theory-of-mind | Hardwick et al. 2020 |
| **Anaesthesia** | The schema can collapse independently of generic arousal | Active research |
| **Illusory ownership** | Manipulating the attention schema (not attention) should change felt experience | Guterstam et al. 2019, *J Neurosci* |
| **Machine consciousness** | An AI that constructs an attention schema will *report* awareness — without metaphysical commitment to it being phenomenal | Graziano 2019 (theoretical) |

## 3. Architecture (Graziano 2020 — "standard model")

AST is *compatible* with — and Graziano explicitly tries to *unify* — several
existing accounts:

```
[ specialised sensory & motor processors ]   ← GWT-style
            ↓ competitive selection (attention)
[ attention apparatus (LIP, SC, PFC, …) ]    ← real, physical attention
            ↓ self-monitoring loop
[ ATTENTION SCHEMA (rTPJ / STS / cingulate) ] ← AST proper
            ↓
[ Reports, beliefs, introspection ]          ← Higher-Order Thought-compatible
            ↓
[ Global broadcast / metacognition ]         ← GWT integration
```

Graziano (2020) explicitly nests AST inside Global Workspace, Higher-Order
Thought, and illusionism (Frankish 2016) to make a "standard model."

## 4. Relation to other theories

- **GWT/GNW:** Compatible. The attention schema is one of the *contents*
  that wins broadcast competition. Awareness reports are the global
  workspace announcing what the schema says. (`../GWT/`)
- **IIT:** Sharply contrasting. IIT says consciousness is identical to a
  physical quantity (Φ); AST says consciousness is the brain's *model of*
  a physical quantity (attention). AST is functionalist; IIT is not.
  (`../IIT/`)
- **PP/FEP:** Highly compatible. The attention schema is precisely a
  precision-allocation model — a generative model over the system's own
  precision dynamics. (`../PP/`, `../FEP/`)
- **Higher-Order Thought (HOT) / Illusionism:** AST is a kind of higher-
  order monitoring theory and is sympathetic to Frankish's illusionism —
  phenomenal qualities are an internally-modelled property, not a
  fundamental one.

## 5. Critiques

1. **Phenomenal/access slip** — Critics argue AST explains only why we
   *report* having experience, not why we *have* it (Chalmers 2018).
   Graziano answers: there is nothing more to explain; the explanandum
   is the report, and the schema is the cause.
2. **Empirical specificity** — Doerig, Schurger & Herzog (2021) note AST
   makes few sharper predictions than other higher-order theories.
3. **rTPJ multiplicity** — rTPJ is implicated in many functions
   (theory of mind, attention reorienting, sense of agency); attributing
   the schema to it is under-constrained.
4. **The "Cartesian theatre" relapse** — does AST sneak in a homunculus
   that *reads* the schema? Graziano insists no: the schema is read by
   downstream processors with no privileged "viewer."
5. **Functionalism vs. consciousness** — If consciousness *is* a schema,
   then any system that constructs the right schema is conscious; IIT
   denies this (the system must have the right Φ). The dispute is
   foundational.
