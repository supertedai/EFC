## Overview

This core-principle note outlines the Energy-Flow Cosmology (EFC) hypothesis that energy flow within galaxy clusters should be observable via galaxy motions, intra-cluster medium (ICM) thermal structure, and gravitational lensing. It proposes using X-ray and lensing observations alongside simulations to relate energy distribution to cluster mass, size, and dark matter, and poses concrete questions and next steps for empirical tests.

## Key Result

Sets out the hypothesis that cluster-scale energy flow should be detectable through galaxy kinematics, ICM thermal gradients, and gravitational lensing, and outlines observational and simulation-based steps to test it; no empirical results are reported.
