## Overview

Introduces Energy-Flow Cosmology (EFC), a framework that posits energy flow (Ef) as the fundamental driver sustaining spacetime, regulating entropy, and modulating an effective speed of light tied to energy-density gradients. The paper replaces the cosmological constant with dynamic energy flow, offers qualitative simulations, and proposes falsifiable predictions for gravitational lensing, redshift in voids, and CMB temperature patterns to address dark energy, the Hubble tension, and CMB anomalies.

## Key Result

Energy flow (Ef) as a dynamical field can replace Λ, linking spacetime stability, entropy growth, and an environment-dependent effective speed of light to explain dark energy, Hubble-tension, and certain CMB anomalies, with concrete observational predictions in halos and voids.
