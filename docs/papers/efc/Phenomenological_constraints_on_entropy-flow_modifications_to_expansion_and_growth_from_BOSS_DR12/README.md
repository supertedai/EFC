## Overview

The paper introduces a single-parameter phenomenological extension to ΛCDM motivated by Energy-Flow Cosmology (EFC), where an entropy-gradient coupling α_{L2} jointly modifies background distances, H(z), and linear growth fσ8(z). Using the BOSS DR12 consensus BAO+RSD measurements with the full 9×9 covariance, it constrains α_{L2} and finds a mild (1.7σ) preference for α_{L2}>0, alongside a characteristic anti-correlation between expansion and growth. The work highlights a sharp, falsifiable prediction η≡Φ/Ψ=1 for linear scales and frames BAO–RSD sign differences as a recasting of the S8 tension in EFC parameter space.

## Key Result

BOSS DR12 yields a mild preference for α_{L2}>0 with α_{L2}=0.35±0.16 and Δχ²=3.01 (1.7σ), indicating anti-correlated changes to expansion and growth consistent with the EFC phenomenology.
