## Overview

Conceptual cosmology proposing that energy flow and the second law of thermodynamics are primary, with time, space, and consciousness emerging from dynamics between two entropy extremes (S=0 “singularity” and S=1 “altular”). It outlines a normalized-entropy framework (0<S<1 “halo”) where observable phenomena occur, linking redshift, time dilation, and the viability of consciousness to energy flow and entropy. The work offers qualitative predictions and directions for testing via cosmological observations and non-equilibrium systems.

## Key Result

Time, space, and consciousness are emergent phenomena maintained by energy flow between entropy extremes, with observable effects such as redshift (as S→1) and time dilation (as S→0).
