## Overview

Proposes an Energy-Flow Cosmology (EFC) framework in which the invariant speed of light c regulates energy flow, spacetime distortion, and entropy across three interacting components: Main Clusters, Small Clusters, and the Halo. The paper outlines qualitative parameter ranges for energy flow and gravitational lensing in each component and reports consistency with Sloan Digital Sky Survey (SDSS) correlations between redshift, lensing, and energy-flow gradients toward the halo.

## Key Result

SDSS redshift and lensing correlations are reported to be consistent with the EFC prediction of decreasing energy flow and increasing spacetime distortion from main clusters through small clusters toward the halo, supporting the role of c as a regulator of energy flow.
