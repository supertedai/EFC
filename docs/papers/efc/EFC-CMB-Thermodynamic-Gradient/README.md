## Overview

Proposes that the CMB (~2.725 K) is an actively maintained thermodynamic equilibrium sustained by a Planck-scale quantum vacuum gradient, rather than relic radiation from a Big Bang. Using an Energy-Entropy framework with a Grid-Higgs amplification of vacuum fluctuations, the paper quantifies a universal energy flow (~2.79×10^35 J/s) and makes falsifiable predictions in CMB anisotropies, nano-Hz gravitational waves, and galaxy clustering.

## Key Result

A Planck-scale vacuum–CMB thermodynamic gradient can sustain the CMB as an active equilibrium and drive cosmic expansion with a quantified energy flow of ~2.79×10^35 J/s, leading to specific, falsifiable observational predictions.
