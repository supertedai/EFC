## Overview

This paper performs a joint Bayesian fit of the EFC coupling α to 14 BAO distance points and 7 fσ8 growth-rate measurements in the Hubble-friction channel, finding α = −1.00 ± 0.46 (2.20σ). It subjects the signal to three robustness controls—sound-horizon variation (N1), σ8 prior sensitivity (N2), and leave-one-out tests over all seven fσ8 points (T7)—and shows the signal is distributed, stable, and not driven by calibration or a single datum.

## Key Result

A joint BAO+growth fit yields α = −1.00 ± 0.46 (2.20σ), robust to sound-horizon variation, σ8 prior choice, and 7/7 leave-one-out tests.
