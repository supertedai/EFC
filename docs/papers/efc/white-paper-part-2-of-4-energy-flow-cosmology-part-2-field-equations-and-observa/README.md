## Overview

Derives the field equations of Energy-Flow Cosmology (EFC) from an entropy-gradient principle, defines the entropy field S(a) and its coupling to the effective gravity parameters µ(k, z) and Σ(k, z), and maps these to observables fσ8, S8, and a regime response surface. The paper presents sealed blind predictions and reports consistency checks against DES Y6, KiDS-1000, DESI DR2, and BOSS DR12, while documenting an erratum that corrects the growth-ODE friction term without affecting reported numerical results.

## Key Result

EFC provides a concrete entropy-field coupling to effective gravity (µ, Σ), a validated mapping to fσ8 and S8, and sealed blind predictions consistent with DES Y6, KiDS-1000, DESI DR2, and BOSS DR12; an erratum corrects the growth-ODE friction term without altering reported inferences.
