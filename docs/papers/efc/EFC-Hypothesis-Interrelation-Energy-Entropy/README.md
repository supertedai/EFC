## Overview

Proposes Energy-Flow Cosmology (EFC), a framework in which universal expansion, structure formation, and phenomena attributed to dark matter/energy emerge from a universal energy Grid composed of Higgs-boson nodes, with energy flow declining linearly as entropy increases. The model treats the speed of light as an emergent property regulated by energy-flow gradients and makes falsifiable predictions in redshift profiles, gravitational lensing, and CMB anisotropies that can distinguish it from ΛCDM.

## Key Result

Introduces a unified hypothesis in which entropy-driven energy flow across a Higgs-node Grid explains cosmic expansion, dark matter phenomenology, and an emergent speed of light, and outlines empirical tests via redshift, lensing, and CMB signatures.
