## Overview

The paper synthesizes the Energy-Flow (EFC) and Grid-Higgs frameworks to propose a cosmology with continuous energy recycling, reinterpreting the CMB as an ongoing process rather than a relic. It replaces dark matter with entropy-driven "entropic tension" between Higgs-anchored spacetime nodes and argues for near-standard behavior except at entropy extremes, offering quantitative, testable deviations in CMB anisotropies and lensing.

## Key Result

Using 10 free parameters (including entropy gradients and Higgs-node density), the model reports χ² ≈ 0 over ℓ = 100–1500 for ACT/WMAP CMB fits and claims natural reproduction of rotation curves and specific, testable deviations in lensing and high-ℓ CMB anisotropies.
