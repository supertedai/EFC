## Overview

Using the COSMOS2025 JWST COSMOS-Web catalog, this paper measures the abundance of massive galaxies (log M*/Msun > 9) at z > 5 and compares them to both forward-modeled expectations and the absolute upper limit implied by the halo mass function assuming ε = 1 baryon-to-star conversion. The authors find that at z > 8, observed galaxy counts exceed even the halo-limited cap by factors of 3–10, with the tension increasing with redshift and remaining robust to extreme assumptions about contamination, mass systematics, and cosmic variance.

## Key Result

At z > 8, observed galaxy abundances (log M*/Msun > 9) in COSMOS-Web exceed the ε = 1 halo-limited cap by factors of 3–10, with the discrepancy strengthening toward higher redshift and remaining under extreme robustness tests.
