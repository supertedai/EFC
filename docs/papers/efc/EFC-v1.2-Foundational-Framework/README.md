## Overview

Presents Energy–Flow Cosmology (EFC), a covariant non-equilibrium thermodynamic field framework that extends General Relativity with a scalar energy-flow potential Ef, disformal matter coupling, and a Lyapunov-consistent dissipation structure. The theory reduces to GR in equilibrium, proposes a parsimonious parameter hierarchy with mild scale dependence, and outlines pre-registered Bayesian cross-prediction tests across rotation curves, weak lensing, SNe-Ia, and BAO. It also provides a functional bridge to variational free energy, enabling continuity to informational and biological systems, and introduces a unique observable: the Entropic Halo Temperature.

## Key Result

Establishes a mathematically well-posed, Lyapunov-consistent, covariant non-equilibrium thermodynamic extension of GR with a single scalar energy-flow field, a parsimonious cross-scale parameter hierarchy, and explicit, pre-registered cross-dataset tests including a unique Entropic Halo Temperature signature.
