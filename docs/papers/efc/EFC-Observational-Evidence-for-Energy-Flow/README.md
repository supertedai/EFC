## Overview

Qualitative synthesis arguing that multiple cosmological observations—redshift-based expansion, CMB anisotropies, gravitational lensing, and galaxy-cluster energetics—are consistent with a hypothesis that energy flow shapes space-time. The piece highlights evidence across scales, from cosmic voids to dark-matter halo edges, and outlines observational fronts where the energy-flow picture could be further tested.

## Key Result

Survey of existing observations indicates that redshift trends, CMB temperature variations, gravitational lensing, and cluster-scale energetics are qualitatively consistent with an energy-flow-driven shaping of space-time and structure formation.
