## Overview

This core-principles paper in Energy-Flow Cosmology argues that universal structures emerge from a dynamic balance between ordering forces (gravity, directed energy flow) and dispersive processes (entropy and expansion). It highlights fractal-like organization and resonance-driven stability as signatures of such balance, illustrated by galaxies, supernovae, and turbulent star-forming clouds, and outlines observational and simulation steps to probe dynamic equilibrium in the cosmos.

## Key Result

Universal structures arise and persist through a dynamic balance between ordering forces (gravity and energy flow) and dispersive processes (entropy and expansion), with resonance enabling stable configurations.
