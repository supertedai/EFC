## Overview

Introduces a regime susceptibility function T(S) that maps the SPARC-calibrated coupling at galactic scales to a cosmological amplitude without new parameters. Shows that β(S)·ρeff(S) is constant, yielding exact self‑regulation, GR recovery as S→0, and a parameter‑free prediction for the amplification βcosm/βgal≈6.25 alongside dynamical dark energy w(a)≠−1 testable by Euclid.

## Key Result

A parameter-free regime susceptibility T(S) maps galactic-scale coupling to cosmological strength, yielding self-regulation (β·ρeff=const), GR recovery at S→0, and a concrete amplification prediction βcosm/βgal≈6.25 with dynamical w(a)≠−1.
