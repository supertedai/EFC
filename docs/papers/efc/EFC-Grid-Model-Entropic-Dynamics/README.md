## Overview

Proposes an Energy-Flow Cosmology (EFC) Grid Model in which gravity emerges from entropy-regulated energy flow rather than from dark matter or a cosmological constant. The paper links entropy, energy-flow gradients, and a dynamic effective gravity to observed rotation curves, gravitational lensing trends, high-ℓ CMB anisotropy deviations, and environment-dependent gravitational-wave propagation, supported by simulations and comparisons to JWST, SDSS/eFEDS, Planck, HST, and LIGO data.

## Key Result

An entropy-regulated energy-flow framework reproduces galaxy rotation trends, predicts an anti-correlation of lensing strength with entropy, allows ~7% high-ℓ CMB deviations, and remains consistent with current gravitational-wave speed limits via environmental dependence, without invoking dark matter or Λ.
