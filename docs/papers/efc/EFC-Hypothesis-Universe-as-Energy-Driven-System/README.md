## Overview

Proposes Energy-Flow Cosmology (EFC), a framework in which energy flow and the second law of thermodynamics govern the emergence and collapse of time, space, and consciousness. Introduces an entropy-scaled state variable S with boundaries at S=0 (singularity) and S=1 ("altular" maximum entropy), and outlines qualitative predictions for redshift behavior, time dilation, and consciousness thresholds.

## Key Result

Time, space, and consciousness are emergent phenomena regulated by energy flow under an entropy-scaled state variable S, with qualitative predictions at S→0 and S→1.

## Sealed Predictions

| ID | Prediction | Falsifiable by |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | As regions approach S → 1 (altular limit), emitted light exhibits increasing redshift correlated with decreasing energy density and rising entropy. | Cross-correlating redshift maps with independent proxies of energy density/entropy gradients near the cosmic edge or low-density voids; absence of correlation would falsify. |
