## Overview

This paper presents Energy-Flow Cosmology (EFC) as a cross-disciplinary framework in which energy flow and entropy are treated as universal variables that generate structure and constrain stability across physical, biological, and socio-technical systems. It synthesizes non-equilibrium thermodynamics, synergetics, and energy-rate density scaling to propose an adaptive window of energy throughput for sustained complexity, and illustrates applications in climate/urban heat, AI systems, and economic networks.

## Key Result

EFC argues that continuous energy flow and entropy production generate geometry and constrain complexity via an adaptive window of energy throughput that applies across physical, biological, and artificial systems.
