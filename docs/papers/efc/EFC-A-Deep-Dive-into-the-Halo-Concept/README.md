## Overview

Conceptual overview of the halo in cosmology, emphasizing its role as a stabilizer and regulator of energy and mass flow around galaxies and clusters. Synthesizes evidence from rotation curves, gravitational lensing, X-ray intracluster emissions, and simulations to motivate halos as dynamic, dark-matter-dominated envelopes that preserve structure and large-scale symmetry within the Energy-Flow Cosmology framing.

## Key Result

Argues that dark-matter-dominated halos act as dynamic regulators and stabilizers of galactic and cluster-scale energy and mass flows, consistent with rotation curves, lensing, and ICM X-ray observations.
