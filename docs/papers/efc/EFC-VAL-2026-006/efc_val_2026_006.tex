\textbf{Plain-language summary.}
The previous paper (VAL-005) reported five non-MCMC results constraining the
EFC parameter $\alpha$. After publication, three of the input data files were
upgraded from synthetic stubs and starter CSVs to DOI-traced observational
data, and a structural code audit identified five biases all pulling toward a
null result for $\alpha$. We document the audit, apply four corrective
patches to the inference daemon, and re-run the full MCMC + non-MCMC stack on
real data. Two principal findings: (1) the headline DESI~Y1
non-MCMC $\Delta\chi^2 = -22.01$ result was conditional on stub-quality DR1
data, and dissolves under DR2 + real $f\sigma_8$; (2) the entropy-gradient
growth variant (VariantH), once tested with a symmetric prior under the
patched daemon, produces a \emph{genuine} null --- not a prior-trap
artifact. The proper status of the EFC mechanism as currently parameterized
is \emph{underdetermined under the present expansion+growth probe stack},
which is distinct from both falsification and confirmation.
Sealed predictions in the GRAV/SPARC/Bullet sectors remain unaffected; sealed
predictions in the $f\sigma_8$/BAO sectors are flagged as conditional on
retired data and require new tests.
\par\smallskip
\textbf{Interim status notice.} The audit also surfaced a structurally open
issue (G2: PPC growth $p$-value $=0.99$) whose root cause has not been
identified in the present session. This corrigendum is therefore explicitly
\emph{interim}: it corrects the public record about the audit and the
VariantH null, but defers a final corrigendum until G2 is resolved (covariance
audit vs.\ PPC-script bug vs.\ genuine model failure --- see \S\ref{sec:g2}).
The VAL-006 framing of ``underdetermined'' is conservative and survives all
three G2 interpretations; no positive growth-sector claim is made.
\end{quote}

The DESI DR2 BAO file (\texttt{bao\_desi\_dr2.json}, 13 points + full

\item \textbf{Five structural biases identified in the inference daemon.}
A code audit on 2026-04-26 found five biases in the autonomous research
daemon, all pulling toward a null result for $\alpha$. Under a binomial
test ($p=0.5$, $k=5$) the asymmetric pattern has $p\approx0.03$. Tier-1
fixes were applied; results below are post-patch.

\item \textbf{Reproducibility audit protocol v2 instituted.} A 43-gate
mechanical correctness audit (\texttt{tools/reproducibility\_audit.py})
covering data provenance, code sync, math sanity, known-bug regression,
numerical sanity, sealed-derivation chain, cycle statistical validity,
and prior drift. The new protocol is required to pass GREEN or YELLOW
before any further publication.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{The four Tier-1 fixes}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item Symmetric prior $\mathcal{U}(-0.5, +0.5)$ for $\bar{S}_0$ (the EFC
  coupling amplitude in VariantH).
\item Walker initialization $\mathcal{U}(-0.15, +0.15)$ so initial
  ensemble straddles zero.
\item $\mu$-clamp lower bound $0.5 \to 0.3$ to permit physical
  suppression solutions.
\item Wire \texttt{posterior\_robustness} and \texttt{null\_test} into
  the daemon's divergence analysis so $\Delta\log L$ values arrive with
  empirical $\sigma$ bands.
\end{enumerate}

The fifth fix --- removing the $+1.0$ magic offset in
\texttt{forbidden\_pattern\_distance.py} --- was applied to remove false
WARNING triggers. Tier-2/3 issues (Axiom~0 secondary status promotion,
VariantG~G10 \texttt{NULL\_TEST\_BY\_DESIGN} reclassification) are
deferred but tracked.

\section{The VariantH genuine null}
\label{sec:varianth}

VariantH is the most direct EFC mechanism test in the suite: the
entropy-gradient growth-suppression chain
$\nabla S \to \mu(a) \to f\sigma_8$ in five free parameters
$(\Omega_m, H_0, \sigma_8, \bar{S}_0, \beta_0)$ with the Hill function
$F = \chi^2/(\chi^2 + \beta_0^2)$. Before the audit, the prior on
$\bar{S}_0$ was $\mathcal{U}(0, 0.5)$, structurally preventing the chain
from finding a suppression solution. After Tier-1 patches (symmetric
prior, straddle-zero walker init, relaxed $\mu$-clamp, wired
robustness), the first valid post-patch cycle reports:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item Posterior on $\bar{S}_0$ \emph{centered near zero} with both tails populated.
\item $\Delta$AIC versus $\Lambda$CDM $\approx +3.9$ (LCDM still wins on parsimony).
\item $\mu$-clamp not engaged (no walls hit).
\item Per-probe $\Delta\log L$ within the calibrated null floor (G3, G4 audit gates).
\end{itemize}

The pre-patch $\Delta$AIC was $+3.9$ as well, but \emph{conditional on a
one-sided prior}. The post-patch $\Delta$AIC is $\approx +3.9$
\emph{conditional on a symmetric prior}. The structural identity of these
numbers is the corrigendum's central technical finding: the original
$+3.9$ was a real null, not a prior-trap. The data does not
\emph{prefer} EFC ($\bar{S}_0\ne 0$), but it also does not
\emph{exclude} it; the posterior is wide enough to contain both
EFC-style mechanisms and pure $\Lambda$CDM.

\paragraph{The proper scientific status.} ``\emph{Underdetermined under
current data + tests}'' --- distinct from \emph{falsified} (data does NOT
exclude the mechanism) and from \emph{confirmed} (data does NOT support a
non-zero amplitude).

\section{Reproducibility audit protocol v2}

We instituted a 43-gate mechanical correctness audit covering:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{A. Data provenance} (5 gates) --- DESI DR2 file present + full cov; DOI traceability of $f\sigma_8$, $H(z)$, Pantheon; no stub references.
\item \textbf{B. Code sync} (6 gates) --- SHA-256 hashes match across the editing repo, the Docker volume-mount repo, and the running container.
\item \textbf{C. Math sanity} (14 gates) --- every variant reduces to $\Lambda$CDM in its parameter limit; clamps engaged correctly; finite $E^2$ at typical parameters.
\item \textbf{D. Known bugs} (4 gates) --- previously identified bugs not regressed.
\item \textbf{E. Numerical sanity} (3 gates) --- BAO and Pantheon covariances positive-definite; $r_d$ safety warnings within tolerance.
\item \textbf{F. Sealed derivation chain} (7 gates) --- freeze provenance complete; freeze data identical at sealed code commit (F4 is historical FAIL for $f\sigma_8$, F5 historical FAIL for DR2 BAO).
\item \textbf{G. Cycle statistical validity} (2 gates) --- PPC artifacts present; PPC growth $p$-value $<0.95$ (currently FAIL at 0.99).
\item \textbf{H. Prior drift} (2 gates) --- emcee and NUTS priors symmetric.
\end{itemize}

Result on 2026-04-28: 39 PASS / 3 FAIL / 1 WARN $\to$ verdict BLOCKED. The
3 FAILs (F4, F5, G2) are documented and bounded: F4 and F5 are historical
facts about retired data; G2 is a known calibration anomaly that blocks
future positive growth-sector claims but does not block this corrigendum
because the corrigendum reports a null/degeneracy, not a positive claim.

\section{The G2 anomaly (PPC growth $p=0.99$) --- structurally open}
\label{sec:g2}

In the post-patch cycle, the posterior predictive check (PPC) on the
growth sector returned $p=0.990$ with calibration scale
$cal_{1\sigma}=1.000$ and offset $\Delta=0.317$. This indicates posterior
predictions for growth observables are systematically far from the
calibrated null distribution. We classify G2 as
\textbf{structurally open}, not deferred --- meaning we have identified the
issue but not isolated its root cause, and the choice among three
distinct interpretations changes how we weight the growth channel in
inference.

\paragraph{Three interpretations, each with different impact.}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{(a) Genuine likelihood mis-specification} (severe). The
$f\sigma_8$ covariance is under-quoted; over-tight likelihood produces
over-extreme PPC. \emph{Consequence:} $\sigma_\alpha$ from the growth
channel is over-confident; the joint posterior is more uncertain than
reported.

\item \textbf{(b) PPC-script bug} (mild). The verification layer itself
has a scale or offset bug. \emph{Consequence:} inference layer is fine;
only the verification number is wrong.

\item \textbf{(c) Genuine model failure} (severe in the opposite
direction). EFC predictions actually do not match observed growth within
correctly quoted covariance. \emph{Consequence:} growth alone may
falsify the present parameterization.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Diagnostic to discriminate.}
Run the same PPC machinery on a known-good model ($\Lambda$CDM with
Planck parameters) against the same $f\sigma_8$ covariance and pseudo-data.
\emph{If $\Lambda$CDM also returns $p\approx0.99$,} the root cause is (b).
\emph{If $\Lambda$CDM returns $p\in[0.05, 0.95]$,} the root cause is (a)
or (c), and discriminating between them requires re-fitting $\alpha$ with
widened $f\sigma_8$ covariance: if $\alpha$-posterior remains near zero,
(a) holds; if posterior shifts substantially, (c) holds.

\paragraph{Why VAL-006 still publishes despite open G2.} The
``underdetermined'' framing is robust to all three interpretations because
the corrigendum makes no positive claim. Under (a) the joint
posterior is wider; under (b) the inference layer is unaffected; under
(c) we already do not claim confirmation. What G2 \emph{does} block is
any subsequent positive growth-sector claim. A final
corrigendum will follow once G2 root cause is identified.

\section{What VAL-006 does and does not assert}

\subsection{Asserts}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item The headline VAL-005 DR1 $\Delta\chi^2 = -22.01$ result was
conditional on stub-quality DR1 data and does not reproduce under DR2 +
real $f\sigma_8$.
\item Five structural code biases existed in the autonomous research
daemon, all pulling toward null. They have been corrected (Tier 1) or
formally tracked (Tier 2/3).
\item Under the corrected daemon and real data, VariantH (the most direct
EFC mechanism test) produces a genuine null: $\Delta\text{AIC}\approx+3.9$
under symmetric prior with both posterior tails populated.
\item A 43-gate reproducibility audit protocol is now in place and
required for all future publications.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Does \emph{not} assert}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item EFC is falsified. (It is not. The posterior includes $\bar{S}_0=0$
and a wide range of non-zero values.)
\item Sealed predictions are wrong. (Sealed predictions are
prereg-protected. Some are conditional on data that has since been
upgraded; the corrigendum makes this explicit but does not retract the
seals.)
\item EFC is confirmed by other observables. (GRAV-KT3v2, SPARC pilot,
BIC pipeline, and Bullet $\delta\kappa$ remain independent open tests
with their own audit gaps.)
\end{enumerate}
