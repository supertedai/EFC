## Overview

Interim corrigendum auditing VAL-005 after upgrading three inputs from stub to DOI-traced observational data and patching five identified biases in the inference daemon. Re-runs show the earlier DESI DR1 non-MCMC Δχ² signal dissolves under DESI DR2 + real fσ8, and the EFC VariantH entropy-gradient growth model yields a genuine null under a symmetric prior and patched pipeline. The paper records Tier-1 fixes, institutes a reproducibility audit protocol, and flags an unresolved growth PPC anomaly (G2), concluding the EFC mechanism is currently underdetermined by the present expansion+growth probe stack.

## Key Result

Under real (DOI-traced) data and patched priors/pipeline, the DESI DR1 non-MCMC signal vanishes and VariantH delivers a genuine null with ΔAIC ≈ +3.9 favoring ΛCDM on parsimony, leaving EFC underdetermined by current probes.
