## Overview

Tests a phenomenological, regime-activated lensing response Σ(k,z) that modifies how matter fluctuations source shear, applied to KiDS-1000 cosmic shear. Finds a large maximum-likelihood improvement over ΛCDM at fixed cosmology that cannot be mimicked by a simple amplitude rescaling, with a distinct low-redshift tomographic signature that reframes the S8 tension as a response effect.

## Key Result

Including a late-time, small-scale lensing response Σ(k,z) improves the KiDS-1000 cosmic shear likelihood by Δ(−2 ln L) ≈ −50.9 at fixed Planck-2018 cosmology, with a distinct low-z tomographic signature and robustness to scale cuts, and cannot be replicated by simple amplitude rescaling.
