## Overview

Pre-registered, cryptographically sealed predictions from Energy-Flow Cosmology (EFC) are provided for the growth-rate observable fσ8(z) and the Hubble distance ratio DH/rd(z) at z = 0.7 and z = 1.0, prior to the unblinding of DESI DR2 full-shape RSD results. The key prediction fσ8(z=0.7)=0.430 contrasts with ΛCDM’s 0.449 (≈2.0σ), with DH/rd also systematically lower than ΛCDM at both redshifts, enabling a direct, falsifiable test with forthcoming DESI DR2 data.

## Key Result

EFC predicts systematically lower growth and distances than ΛCDM, with fσ8(z=0.7)=0.430 vs 0.449 (≈2.0σ) and DH/rd lower at z=0.7 and z=1.0 (2.3σ and 3.1σ separations), sealed prior to DESI DR2 unblinding.
