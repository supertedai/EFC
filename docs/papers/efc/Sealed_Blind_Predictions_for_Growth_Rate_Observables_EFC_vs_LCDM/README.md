## Overview

This paper presents pre-registered, cryptographically sealed predictions from Energy-Flow Cosmology (EFC) for the large-scale structure growth observable fσ8(z) and the Hubble distance ratio DH/rd(z) at z = 0.7 and z = 1.0, prior to DESI DR2 full-shape RSD unblinding. Two independent NUTS-MCMC freezes predict suppressed growth relative to ΛCDM (e.g., fσ8(0.7)=0.430 vs 0.449), providing falsifiable, multi-observable tests once DR2 is released.

## Key Result

Sealed EFC predictions indicate suppressed late-time growth relative to ΛCDM, with fσ8(0.7)=0.430 vs 0.449 (2.0σ), and DH/rd lower than ΛCDM at z=0.7 and z=1.0 (2.3–3.1σ separations), to be tested by DESI DR2 full-shape RSD/BAO.
