## Overview

Analytical confrontation of Energy-Flow Cosmology (EFC) with three JWST-era Bullet Cluster mass reconstructions: highest-resolution mass map, spectroscopically anchored lens model, and a revised ~10:1 minor-merger mass ratio. Paper tightens EFC’s structural constraints, registers the A_sig shock-front asymmetry test, reports a null baseline on a PIEMD model, and assesses qualitative consistency of EFC’s regime-transition (mu≈1.1, Sigma≈1.2) with new profiles without introducing new parameters.

## Key Result

JWST-era reconstructions and the confirmed ~10:1 merger tighten EFC’s structural constraints; the pre-registered A_sig pipeline’s PIEMD baseline yields a null (A_sig ≈ −6.9×10^−4, p=0.98), while regime-transition values (mu≈1.1, Sigma≈1.2) show qualitative consistency but remain insufficient alone to explain the full mass discrepancy.
