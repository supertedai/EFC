## Overview

Proposes Energy–Flow Cosmology (EFC), in which dark matter, dark energy, and the CMB are thermodynamic phases of a single Grid–Higgs medium governed by an energy–flux field coupled to local entropy. The paper identifies convergent (DM), divergent (DE), and isothermal interface (CMB) regimes and outlines testable predictions linking CMB anisotropies, large‑scale structure, and the dark‑energy equation of state.

## Key Result

DM, DE, and the CMB emerge as convergent, divergent, and isothermal-interface phases of a single entropy-coupled energy–flow field embedded in a Grid–Higgs medium.
