## Overview

Analyzes three SDSS DR18 galaxy clusters to study how energy flow diminishes with redshift while entropy increases, incorporating spacetime distortion and gravitational lensing corrections. Reports a best-fit power-law index n = −0.33 for energy-flow evolution, a small negative spacetime-distortion parameter α = −0.059, and a lensing factor of 0.636, with cluster entropies remaining homogeneous. The study argues these trends are consistent with stable halo dynamics across clusters.

## Key Result

Energy flow in clusters declines with redshift (n = −0.33) after modeling spacetime distortion (α = −0.059) and lensing (factor = 0.636), while entropy increases uniformly, indicating stable halo dynamics.
