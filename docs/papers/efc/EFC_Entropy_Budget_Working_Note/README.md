# The Cosmic Entropy Budget as Constraint on the EFC S-Field

## Summary

The observed cosmic entropy budget -- dominated by supermassive black holes
(S_SMBH ~ 10^104 k_B) -- is structurally consistent with EFC's S-field.
SMBHs sit at S = S_max (grid collapse boundary), while the CMB approaches
S_max from the thermal direction. The S(z) transition at z ~ 1 plausibly
tracks the decline of AGN entropy production ("thermostat conjecture").

## Key Results

- **Entropy hierarchy:** S_SMBH >> S_stellar >> S_CMB maps onto EFC regimes
- **SMBH at S_max:** Bekenstein-Hawking S = S_max (grid collapse in GRC)
- **CMB at S_max:** Thermal maximum from opposite direction
- **Thermostat conjecture:** AGN activity decline drives S(z) sigmoid at z ~ 1
- **Consistency relation:** S_EFC/S_max = S_BH/S_BH,max(M) (Eq. 2)

## Predictions

- **P1:** SMBH density correlates with local mu (RAR residuals with/without AGN)
- **P2:** S(z) tracks AGN luminosity density (inflection at z ~ 1-2)
- **P3:** Void galaxies have enhanced mu (stronger g_obs/g_bar)
- **P4:** S_max scales with Bekenstein-Hawking limit for typical SMBH mass

## Open Gaps

- **G1:** Quantitative S(z)-AGN derivation from action
- **G2:** S-field normalization (requires grid-mode density of states)
- **G3:** Interior description (L3 extension needed inside horizon)
- **G4:** Hawking radiation and S-field decay
