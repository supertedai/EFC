## Overview

This working note argues that the observed cosmic entropy budget—overwhelmingly dominated by supermassive black holes (SMBHs)—is structurally consistent with Energy-Flow Cosmology (EFC). It maps Bekenstein–Hawking entropy to the EFC S-field normalization, places SMBHs at S = S_max (grid-collapse boundary) while the CMB approaches S_max from the thermal side, and proposes that the decline of AGN accretion drives the sigmoid transition in S(z) near z ~ 1.

## Key Result

SMBH-dominated cosmic entropy is consistent with EFC by identifying black holes at S = S_max and linking the S(z) sigmoid transition near z ~ 1 to the decline in AGN accretion-driven entropy production.
