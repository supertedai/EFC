## Overview

Proposes that the universal light-speed limit (c) is an emergent property of underlying energy-flow dynamics, rather than a primary boundary condition. It frames c as meaningful only within regimes where energy gradients sustain space-time, becoming irrelevant at the entropy extremes S=0 (singularity) and S=1 (ultimate dispersion), with maximal dynamical complexity at intermediate entropy.

## Key Result

The paper argues that the light-speed limit is an emergent consequence of energy-flow constraints that sustain space-time, losing operational relevance at the entropy extremes S=0 and S=1, with peak dynamical complexity near S≈0.5.
