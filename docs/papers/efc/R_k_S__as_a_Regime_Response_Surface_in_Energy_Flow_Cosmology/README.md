## Overview

Introduces a state-dependent gravitational response surface R(k,S) that extends EFC phenomenology from time/redshift dependence to dependence on a structural maturity variable S. The paper unifies prior µ(a) and µ(k,z) approaches, defines S operationally from the nonlinear-to-linear variance ratio, and formulates a falsifiable framework where a single global surface must describe all late-universe probes. It specifies DES Y6-oriented falsification criteria and posits that the L1→L2 transition explains the S8 tension.

## Key Result

A single, structure-dependent response surface R(k,S) with µ≈1 at S≈0 and µ>1 for S>0 can unify late-universe gravitational deviations and attributes the S8 tension to the L1→L2 transition rather than probe-specific systematics.
