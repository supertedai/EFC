## Overview

Proposes Energy-Flow Cosmology (EFC), the hypothesis that a globally conserved yet locally dynamic energy flow—modulated by entropy and matter distribution—acts as the scaffolding that sustains and shapes spacetime curvature. The paper outlines a research program to relate energy-flow and entropy gradients to observed gravitational phenomena across scales and calls for simulations and comparisons to gravitational lensing and CMB data. Its contribution is a conceptual framework and test agenda rather than new empirical results.

## Key Result

Articulates the hypothesis that global energy flow, locally modulated by entropy and matter distribution, sustains and shapes spacetime curvature across scales.
