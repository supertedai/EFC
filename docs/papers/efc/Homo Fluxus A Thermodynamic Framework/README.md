## Overview

Proposes a thermodynamic substrate for cognition in which local entropy minimization through coherent information flow underlies consciousness, affect, social ethics, and AI alignment. Introduces Homo Fluxus (agent-as-node-in-flow) and systemic empathy (field-coupled coherence sensitivity), and predicts cross-modal covariation of coherence proxies as observable signatures of a single coherence potential.

## Key Result

A single thermodynamic coherence variable governs consciousness and systemic empathy, predicting a recoverable cross-modal latent factor linking integrated information rate, large-scale neural synchrony, and interoceptive–allostatic integration.
