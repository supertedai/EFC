## Overview

Proposes a minimal effective field theory completion for Energy-Flow Cosmology (EFC) in two formulations: a Horndeski-like scalar–tensor EFT and a vector–tensor EFT with a dynamical energy-flow 4-vector. Both are driven by a sigmoid entropy field S(a) that ensures GR recovery at high redshift and targets a mid‑z signature with weakened growth (mu < 1), gravitational slip (eta > 1), and enhanced lensing (Sigma > 1). The note defines the observables, derives the slip mechanism from entropy-gradient anisotropic stress, and specifies stability/consistency conditions, with numerical evaluation deferred to hi_class/EFTCosmoMC.

## Key Result

A minimal EFC EFT driven by a sigmoid entropy field can, in principle, realize the target mid‑z signature (mu < 1, eta > 1, Sigma > 1) either via Horndeski-like braiding/Planck-mass running or via entropy-gradient anisotropic stress in a vector–tensor completion, subject to stability and CMB-compatibility constraints and pending numerical validation.
