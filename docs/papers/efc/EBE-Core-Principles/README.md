## Overview

Foundational specification of Entropy-Bounded Empiricism (EBE), a regime-aware epistemic framework for interpreting measurements in entropy- and energy-flow-governed systems. It introduces the S-axis (physical regime hierarchy) and L-axis (epistemic layer hierarchy) and formalizes the Regime-Consistent Measurement Protocol (RCMP) to prevent regime leakage and interpretive overreach. The paper codifies regime gating and proxy-chain transparency to make assumptions traceable and claims comparable across regimes.

## Key Result

EBE establishes a regime-aware mapping between physical entropy regimes (S-axis) and epistemic layers (L-axis) via RCMP, enforcing regime-gated interpretation and proxy-chain transparency to prevent interpretive overreach.
