## Overview

Outlines how Energy-Flow Cosmology (EFC) posits entropy as a primary driver of cosmic expansion and structure, linking accelerating expansion and galactic clustering/dispersion to entropy dynamics. Introduces boundary states S=0 (singularity) and S=1 (altularity) as limiting regimes for time–space behavior, and highlights observational venues—CMB, gravitational waves, and edge-of-universe “Halo” observations—to probe these claims and their practical implications across disciplines.

## Key Result

Conceptual claim that increasing entropy catalyzes the universe’s accelerating expansion and governs structure formation and limiting time–space regimes (S=0, S=1).
