## Overview

Five-phase localization study of where late-time modified-gravity signals can reside, using Energy-Flow Cosmology (EFC) as a case study. The background gate collapses (alpha consistent with zero) under joint CMB+BAO+SNe, pure growth modification (mu<1 with Sigma=1) is blocked by CMB lensing, and a narrow perturbation-sector slip window survives Planck with mu≈0.94 and Sigma≈1.05. The surviving valley is degenerate with the Planck AL anomaly, but fσ8 growth data independently favor mu<1, providing a growth-sector sign constraint.

## Key Result

The background channel is observationally empty (α → 0), pure growth-modification is excluded by the CMB lensing barrier, and a narrow perturbation-sector slip window survives Planck at (µ≈0.94, Σ≈1.05), degenerate with AL but supported in sign by fσ8.
