% =========================================================
% ABSTRACT
% =========================================================
\begin{infobox}
\textbf{Abstract.} We present non-MCMC constraints on the EFC parameter $\alpha$ from five independent datasets: DESI Y1 BAO, BOSS DR12 BAO, multi-epoch growth rate ($f\sigma_8$), IllustrisTNG cluster statistics, and cosmic chronometer $H(z)$. \textbf{Non-MCMC results are treated strictly as model-comparison metrics, not as parameter posteriors.} Whereas concurrent MCMC inference is degeneracy-limited ($\alpha = -0.101 \pm 0.212$, $0.479\sigma$, all chains terminating in \texttt{STOPPED\_DEGENERACY\_PERSISTS}), cross-validation and likelihood-ratio methods yield consistent directional preference for EFC across the geometric and growth probes ($\Delta\chi^2_{\mathrm{DESI}} = -22.01$; $\Delta\chi^2_{\mathrm{BOSS}} = -7.77$; $\alpha = -1.00 \pm 0.46$ at $2.20\sigma$ from $f\sigma_8$). Non-MCMC results lie within $\sim 0.7\sigma$ of the previously sealed blind predictions ($\alpha = -0.702, -0.689$), while the MCMC posterior shows a $\sim 2.8\sigma$ tension with the same priors. We interpret this as methodological tension between inference frameworks under degeneracy, not as a detection-level signal. The note formalises results previously held only in an internal breakthrough tracker, registers two new validation IDs (DESI Y1, IllustrisTNG), and cross-references prior validations \texttt{EFC-VAL-2026-002} and \texttt{EFC-VAL-2026-003}.
\end{infobox}

% =========================================================
% 1. MOTIVATION
% =========================================================
\section{Motivation}

The EFC parameter $\alpha$ governs the entropic correction to the cosmological background equation (sector L0--L2 in the EFC framework atlas). Standard MCMC inference (\texttt{cobaya} with EMCEE / NUTS samplers, ten consecutive runs as of April 2026) repeatedly terminates with the diagnostic \texttt{STOPPED\_DEGENERACY\_PERSISTS}: the posterior for $\alpha$ remains uninformative ($\alpha = -0.101 \pm 0.212$, significance $0.479\sigma$, $\Delta\mathrm{AIC} = +1.763$), with all internal node states reported as \texttt{COLLAPSED}, \texttt{FAIL}, or \texttt{NO\_SIGNAL}.

Non-MCMC inference --- using $\Delta\chi^2$ likelihood ratios, $k$-fold cross-validation (CV), leave-one-out (LOO) validation, and information-criterion comparison ($\Delta\mathrm{AIC}$) --- evaluates model fit and generalisation directly, without sampling the posterior. These methods constitute model comparison metrics rather than parameter estimates, and they are not vulnerable to the same parameter degeneracies that paralyse MCMC chains.

This note formalises five non-MCMC results that previously existed only in an internal breakthrough tracker, registers them as system-level validation entries, and compares them against the framework's pre-registered sealed predictions.

\begin{warnbox}
\textbf{Interpretive caveat.} Non-MCMC results in this note are model-comparison metrics computed under fixed-form likelihood evaluation and cross-validation. They are \emph{not} marginalised posterior estimates and should not be interpreted as such. Statistical comparisons against MCMC posteriors are reported as inference-framework tensions, not as posterior contradictions.
\end{warnbox}

\subsection{Consistency analysis}

Treating the sealed mean $\bar\alpha_{\mathrm{seal}} = -0.696$ as a reference point and using the reported uncertainties:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=18pt,itemsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{\texttt{fs8\_extended} vs.\ sealed mean:} offset $= 0.304$; in units of the LOO uncertainty $\sigma_{\mathrm{LOO}} = 0.46$, this corresponds to $0.66\sigma_{\mathrm{LOO}}$. \emph{Consistent.}
  \item \textbf{MCMC vs.\ sealed mean:} offset $= 0.595$; in units of the MCMC uncertainty $\sigma_{\mathrm{MCMC}} = 0.212$, this corresponds to $2.81\sigma_{\mathrm{MCMC}}$. \emph{Tension under MCMC framing.}
  \item \textbf{MCMC vs.\ \texttt{fs8\_extended}:} offset $= 0.899$; under quadrature combination of uncertainties ($\sqrt{0.46^2 + 0.212^2} = 0.506$), this corresponds to a cross-framework offset of $1.78\sigma$.
\end{itemize}

\begin{warnbox}
\textbf{Methodological note.} The cross-framework offset above compares a posterior-derived $\sigma$ (MCMC) against a CV-derived uncertainty (LOO). These are \emph{not the same statistical object}: the former is a marginalised credible width, the latter a generalisation-based scatter. The tension should be read as \emph{a methodological signal that the two inference frameworks disagree under the present degeneracy condition}, not as a posterior-level contradiction. Earlier internal versions of this comparison cited a $\sim 4\sigma$ figure; that figure conflated inference frames and is corrected here.
\end{warnbox}

\subsection{Interpretation (conservative, protocol v1.2)}

The non-MCMC result and the sealed predictions are mutually consistent within $1\sigma_{\mathrm{LOO}}$. The MCMC result and the sealed predictions are in tension at the $\sim 2.8\sigma_{\mathrm{MCMC}}$ level. The combined pattern is consistent with the hypothesis that the MCMC chains are degeneracy-limited rather than expressing the absence of an $\alpha$-signal. This is an indication, not a detection.

\section{Conclusion}

Non-MCMC inference across BAO, growth-rate, and cluster datasets yields consistent directional preference for EFC in three of five probes, all sub-$3\sigma$ but mutually agreeing in sign and order of magnitude with the previously sealed predictions ($\bar\alpha_{\mathrm{seal}} = -0.696$ versus $\alpha_{\mathrm{LOO}} = -1.00 \pm 0.46$). The concurrent MCMC posterior is degeneracy-limited and agnostic on the same data. The methodological tension between MCMC and non-MCMC frameworks is interpreted as a signature of unresolved degeneracy in the posterior sampling, not as evidence for or against EFC at the detection level.

\subsection{Next steps}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=22pt,itemsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{MCMC informed by non-MCMC.} Use the LOO-derived $\alpha$-direction as a profile-likelihood axis to break the posterior degeneracy without imposing a strong prior.
  \item \textbf{Joint fit.} Combine BAO and $f\sigma_8$ likelihoods in a single inference run with the proposed profile axis to test signal robustness.
  \item \textbf{Cluster audit.} Re-evaluate the IllustrisTNG entry under explicit DOF accounting before any further weight is placed on the $\chi^2_{\mathrm{red}} = 0$ result.
  \item \textbf{Euclid DR1 (Oct 2026).} The sealed \texttt{phi\_psi\_slip} kill-test (watchlist W2) provides the next external falsification opportunity; pipeline preparation is in scope of the EFC roadmap.
\end{enumerate}
