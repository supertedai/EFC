## Overview

Formalizes a general, validity-aware measurement framework in which analysis is cartography—mapping outputs on a structured response surface—rather than a single verdict. It introduces five operative concepts (regime, proxy, placement, episenter, compression) and applies them to two domains (cosmology via WP3/R(k,S) and AI evaluation), deriving falsifiable predictions and criteria that distinguish regime-stable claims from locally coherent artifacts.

## Key Result

Across cosmology (WP3/R(k,S)) and AI evaluation, apparent model conflicts are shown to be regime mismatches; proxy choice and observer placement systematically reshape residual structure, supporting a cartographic approach to measurement with explicit falsifiable predictions.
