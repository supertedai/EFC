## Overview

Concept paper in Energy-Flow Cosmology (EFC) proposing that the universe’s boundary conditions are set by two extremes: singularities (T=0), where spacetime collapses, and ultimate dispersion (T=1), where entropy is maximal and structures dissolve. It argues that cosmic structure emerges from energy flow dynamically balancing between these limits and outlines observational and modeling avenues (black holes, expansion/voids) to probe dynamics near the extremes.

## Key Result

The paper posits that spacetime ceases to exist at two energy-flow-defined extremes—singularities (T=0) and ultimate dispersion (T=1)—with cosmic structure maintained by dynamic energy flow between them.
