## Overview

Introduces Energy-Flow Cosmology (EFC) as a theoretical framework where an energy-flow vector field interacts with entropy and standard relativistic boundary conditions to shape space-time dynamics. The work proposes integrating energy-flow effects into Einstein’s field equations and outlines qualitative predictions for cosmic expansion in low-density regions, black hole horizon stability, and temporal coherence of quantum fluctuations.

## Key Result

Proposes a theoretical EFC framework where an energy-flow vector field coupled to entropy modifies space-time dynamics and yields qualitative predictions for cosmic void acceleration, black hole horizon stability, and quantum-scale temporal coherence.
