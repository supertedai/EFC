## Overview

Sealed pre-registration of an Energy-Flow Cosmology (EFC) prediction for the high-redshift (z=7–12) halo mass function n(M,z) derived via the µ(a) perturbation channel and a gated background. The work locks the structural chain g(a) → µ(a) → D(a) → σ(M,z) → n(M,z), specifies datasets (JWST CEERS/COSMOS-Web/GLASS), HMF prescriptions (Press–Schechter, Sheth–Tormen, Tinker 2008/2010), and explicit falsification thresholds, to be executed once (B, zt, n) are fixed by a joint S(z) fit.

## Key Result

A sealed, falsifiable EFC prediction for n(M,z) at z=7–12 is registered via the µ(a) channel with explicit pass/fail criteria and three parallel HMF prescriptions; numerical curves will be produced once (B, zt, n) are fixed by the joint S(z) fit.

| P3 | Robustness across HMF modeling: Press–Schechter, Sheth–Tormen, and Tinker 2008/2010 computed from the same σ(M,z) must give a consistent verdict regarding agreement with data. | Simultaneous (>3σ) failure of all three prescriptions to match JWST counts once parameters are fixed by the S(z) fit. |
