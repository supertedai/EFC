## Overview

This core EFC principle argues that the speed of light is the maximum permissible rate of energy flow in space-time, thereby enforcing causality and structuring cosmic dynamics. It motivates this limit via relativity, mass–energy equivalence, and observed phenomena such as time dilation, gravitational lensing, and cosmic horizons, and outlines open questions about the interplay of c with universal expansion and dark energy.

## Key Result

Within EFC, c is posited as the universal maximum rate of energy flow in space-time, enforcing causality and explaining relativistic effects near light speed.
