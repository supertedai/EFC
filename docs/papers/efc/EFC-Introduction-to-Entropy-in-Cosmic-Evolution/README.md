## Overview

Introductory overview of entropy’s role in cosmic evolution within the Energy-Flow Cosmology (EFC) framework. It outlines how entropy underpins the arrow of time, informs the formation of large-scale structure and black hole thermodynamics, and guides the universe toward thermodynamic equilibrium, linking these themes to energy flow and time–space dynamics.

## Key Result

Entropy is presented as the directional driver of cosmic evolution, shaping structure formation, black-hole thermodynamics, and the universe’s progression toward thermodynamic equilibrium within the EFC framework.
