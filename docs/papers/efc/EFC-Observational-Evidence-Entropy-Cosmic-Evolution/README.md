## Overview

This paper surveys observational indicators that entropy gradients play a central role in cosmic evolution. It synthesizes evidence from the CMB, galaxy clusters, the cosmic web, and black hole environments, and argues that entropy gradients modulate structure formation and local space-time expansion/compression.

## Key Result

Multi-band observations of the CMB, galaxy clusters, and large-scale structure are consistent with entropy gradients shaping structure growth and modulating local space-time expansion and compression.
