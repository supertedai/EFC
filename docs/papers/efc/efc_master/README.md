## Overview

Formal master specification of Energy-Flow Cosmology (EFC), defining the dynamical energy-flow field, an effective potential coupled to entropy, an entropy-modulated expansion rate, and an entropy-dependent effective light speed. The paper unifies rotation curves, lensing behavior, and late-time acceleration as emergent thermodynamic effects without invoking non-baryonic dark matter or an external dark-energy component.

## Key Result

Defines a thermodynamically coupled dynamical-structural-light propagation framework where rotation curves, lensing profiles, and cosmic acceleration emerge from the fields (E_f, S) and c(S) without dark matter or external dark energy.
