## Overview

Reports controlled scans of the sealed EFC lensing engine showing that the kinetic stiffness K0 dominantly sets the redshift of the predicted sign-changing Σeff(z) crossover, while amplitude parameters have minor impact. Documents cross-sector consistency (µ < 1) between sealed lensing and growth sectors, establishes robustness to power-spectrum choice, and pre-registers joint falsification tests for Euclid DR1 and DESI DR2.

## Key Result

The sealed EFC lensing engine robustly predicts a sign-changing, non-monotonic Σeff(z) with crossover near z ≈ 0.44, dominantly controlled by K0 (Δz ≈ 0.87 for K0 ∈ [0.5,4.0]) and only weakly by amplitude parameters (Δz ≈ 0.11 for ×50 rescaling), with the sign-change robust to power-spectrum choice (BBKS cross-check shifts ≤ 0.06) and consistent µ < 1 across lensing and growth sectors.
