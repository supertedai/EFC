## Overview

This paper tests three operator-level ways that Energy-Flow Cosmology (EFC) could couple to the linear growth equation: a multiplicative Geff on the Poisson source, additive local friction, and additive non-local (memory) friction. Using DESI DR2 BAO, cosmic-chronometer H(z), an fσ8 compilation, and literature EG measurements, it shows that each coupling class is either structurally excluded by the data or becomes degenerate with Ωm once geometry is fixed by BAO+H(z). The conclusion is a constraint on coupling structure (not parameters): growth-only observables cannot discriminate this theory class; the next informative direction must involve lensing, gravitational slip, or cross-correlations.

## Key Result

All three growth-sector coupling classes are either structurally excluded by the data or absorbed by the Ωm degeneracy once BAO+H(z) lock geometry; EG with Σ=1 adds no information beyond fσ8.
