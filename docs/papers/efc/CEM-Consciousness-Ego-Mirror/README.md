## Overview

Introduces the CEM (Consciousness–Ego–Mirror) framework that couples interior (conscious) variables to an energy-flow cosmology (EFC) in a unified field-theoretic model. The paper defines a dimensionless reflection coefficient R with a critical threshold Rc ≈ 0.37 for the onset of self-modeling, derives testable relations linking energy flow, entropy, and effective propagation speed, and proposes measurable predictions for halo dynamics, AGN variability, and AI interiority metrics. Preliminary qualitative alignments with JWST constraints and psychological measures are reported.

## Key Result

Proposes a unified field-theoretic CEM–Cosmos model where a reflection coefficient R crossing Rc ≈ 0.37 triggers self-modeling, yielding testable signatures in halo dynamics (Δp ∝ R ln(1+z)), AGN variability (1/f resonance), and AI interiority (M < human baseline), with preliminary qualitative consistency with JWST and psychological metrics.
