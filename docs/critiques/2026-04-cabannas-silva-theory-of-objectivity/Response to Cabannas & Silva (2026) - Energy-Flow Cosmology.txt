# Response to Cabannas & Silva (2026): "Energy-Flow Cosmology and the
# Modal Discipline of Objectivity"

Morten Magnusson, Symbiose Research
ORCID: 0009-0002-4860-5095
Date: April 2026

I thank Cabannas and Silva for their critical–propositional engagement
with EFC v2.1. This is, to my knowledge, the first external commentary
on the framework, and I welcome it on principle: cosmological proposals
of this scope benefit from being read against alternative ontological
disciplines.

I will respond on three points only.

## 1. On the operational bridges (Section 13)

The eight bridges proposed by the authors largely coincide with the
empirical programme already underway in EFC. Specifically:

- Bridge 2 (galactic rotation curves from k(S), V(E_f, S)) is addressed
  in *Empirical Validation of Energy-Flow Cosmology: Cluster Lensing and
  Galaxy Rotation Curves* (2026).
- Bridge 3 (entropic halos vs. gravitational lensing) is addressed in
  the Bullet Cluster EFC analysis using Clowe et al. (2006) and HSC
  weak-lensing data, and extended in *A Regime-Activated Lensing
  Response Improves the Fit to KiDS-1000 Cosmic Shear*
  (doi:10.6084/m9.figshare.31271917).

Bridges 5, 6, 7 and 8 — local Lorentz consistency of c(S), a quantitative
CMB account, the ontological separation of information / radiation /
memory / cognition, and falsifiability criteria for EFC-C — remain open
problems within the EFC programme. I accept them as legitimate.

## 2. On the philosophical framework

The authors propose that EFC be reinterpreted within the vocabulary of
the Theory of Objectivity: S=0 as Nothingness, S=1 as the necessary
non-element, the Grid–Higgs field as singular aura, energy flow as
logical currents. I take this as a constructive proposal for ontological
dialogue, not as a binding requirement.

EFC's commitment is to formalisations whose validity is settled by
observational confrontation rather than by axiomatic translation.
The two programmes need not converge at the level of vocabulary in
order to share concerns about unification, the status of dark
components, and the place of cognition in physical reality.

## 3. On the evaluation

I record the score (8.2/10) without further comment, as scores of this
kind are necessarily subjective and depend on the scoring framework.
EFC's progress will be measured against data, not against scales.

I welcome continued engagement, particularly on the four open bridges
above.
