First external commentary on Energy-Flow Cosmology (v2.1).

## Summary

The authors evaluate EFC v2.1 from within their own framework
(*Theory of Objectivity*) and propose a vocabulary translation
(S=0 as Nothingness, S=1 as necessary non-element, Grid–Higgs as
singular aura, energy flow as logical currents). They award EFC 8.2/10
and propose eight operational bridges in their Section 13.

## Status

- Not peer reviewed (Zenodo self-published, AI-assisted text).
- Bridges 2 and 3 are already addressed in existing EFC empirical work
  (Bullet Cluster, KiDS-1000 cosmic shear, galaxy rotation curves).
- Bridges 5–8 (local Lorentz consistency of c(S), quantitative CMB,
  ontological separation of information/radiation/memory/cognition,
  falsifiability of EFC-C) are accepted as open problems.
- The Theory-of-Objectivity vocabulary translation is treated as a
  proposal for dialogue, not as a binding constraint.

See `response-2026-04.md` for the public reply.
